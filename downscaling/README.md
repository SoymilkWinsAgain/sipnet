# SIPNET Downscaling

Predict net ecosystem exchange (NEE) and latent heat flux (LE) on a requested
grid window using a model bundle and environmental covariates.

- [Make your first map](../docs/user-guide/downscaling.md#make-your-first-map) with the shared model and a 16 × 16 pixel example.
- [Train from SIPNET and tower data](../docs/user-guide/downscaling.md#train-from-sipnet-and-tower-data) to produce ensemble targets, training arrays and your own model bundle.
- [Find the inputs](../docs/user-guide/downscaling-data.md) on BU SCC or obtain them from the listed providers.
